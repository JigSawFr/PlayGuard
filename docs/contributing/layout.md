## The Cadran look

The pieces every screen builds with are [Aurore](https://github.com/JigSawFr/Aurore)'s (`borealis/cadran/ui/`: `cadran_kit`, `cadran_modal`, `cadran_frame`, `cadran_list`), which PlayGuard tells what it needs through `ui::hooks` (`source/activity/screen_hooks.cpp`: the main screen and its tabs, the rail's icons, PlayGuard's version, logo and pill states, saving the look's preferences): a colour is always a key of the palettes (`kit::color("cadran/teal")`, `@theme/cadran/…` in the XML), never a value of its own, so both themes follow; corners and type sizes are the kit's tokens (`kit::RADIUS_*`, `@style/cadran/radius/*`, `kit::TEXT_*`). A dialog is `ui::dialog` / `ui::confirm` / `ui::confirm_danger` / `ui::info` (anything under its text through `ui::dialog(text, below)`), a failure `ui::error` (a dialog: it stays until read), a refusal `ui::refuse` (a toast), a list to pick from `ui::pick` (`checked` false for things to do, or `ui::pick_actions`). What deletes, discards or stops confirms in coral (`confirm_danger`, `Emphasis::DANGER`); the confirmation is the last button.

The controller's keys mean the same everywhere: Ⓐ opens or confirms, Ⓑ goes back or cancels, Ⓧ reads again (*Refresh*) or the screen's own second action, said in the footer; Ⓨ is the notification bell from the main screen (a screen of its own may give it its main action, said in its footer); Ⓛ / Ⓡ move between a screen's tabs or by 5 min; **ZR** *Undo*; ⊖ the language, theme and text size.
